A page describing a book I read

I read the trilogy Aléxandros by Valerio Massimo Manfredi. In another language, the title can be written as Alexander Trilogy. The story follows Alexander the Great from his youth to the edge of the known world.

The trilogy is composed of three novels: Il figlio del sogno, Le sabbie di Amon, and Il confine del mondo. If you want a short overview of the plot and editions, you can read this publisher page .

“History is memory: the attempt of humanity to create a common memory.” — Valerio Massimo Manfredi
