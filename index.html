My Book Review Webpage

A simple page describing a book I read

I recently read Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone by J. K. Rowling. In Italian, the title is Harry Potter e la Pietra Filosofale. It’s a fantasy novel that follows Harry as he discovers he is a wizard and begins his first year at Hogwarts.

One part I enjoyed is how the story mixes everyday life with magical elements. If you want to read more about the story setting, you can visit this official Wizarding World webpage (this link is connected to a fragment of text).
